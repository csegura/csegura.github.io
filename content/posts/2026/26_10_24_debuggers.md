# MSDOS Debuggers

In ancient times we didn't have sophisticated disassemblers and debuggers for DOS programs like nowadays. Remeber using Sourcer to inspect and understand the code, line by line and instruction by instruction. Triyin to find out how it worked and why it behaved the way it did was a common task.

Today, we have powerful tools, to reverse engineer almost everything, I still find myself needing to understand legacy DOS binaries at a low level, why? I don´t know but I suppose that I spent so much time with them in the past and developed a fascination for their inner workings, also some pices of software I never were able to fully comprehend back then.
